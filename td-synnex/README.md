# TD SYNNEX research

Materials for the executive meeting on the future of TD SYNNEX's commercial organization and AI (2030 vision).

- `site/index.html` — the briefing as a website, with charts and diagrams. Built from the report by `site/src/build.py` (run `python3 site/src/build.py`; needs the `markdown` package). Published privately at https://claude.ai/artifact/No9UDigmWzJyCfoJrsyGpP.
- `reports/TD SYNNEX commercial AI vision 2030.md` — the full research report. Updated Oct 12, 2026 with the Q3 FY26 10-Q. Section 12 holds preliminary 2030 transformation recommendations; a review log of 10-Q corrections sits at the end.
- `data/q3fy26_data.json` — chart data, each block labelled by source (10-Q, derived, or secondary).
- `sources/` — the Form 10-Q for the quarter ended Aug 31, 2026 (PDF and text extract).
- `research_notes/TD SYNNEX commercial AI vision 2030/` — sourced notes behind the report, one file per topic:
  - `10q_q3_fy26_primary.md` — fact base from the 10-Q, with page numbers
  - `business_overview_financials.md` — scale, leadership, FY2024–FY2026 financials, capital allocation
  - `product_mix_and_verification.md` — Endpoint/Advanced/Hyve split, estimated category mix, fact checks
  - `strategy_growth_ai.md` — strategic priorities, growth technologies, Destination AI, StreamOne, M&A
  - `go_to_market_channel.md` — sales organization, partner programs, platforms, financing, services
  - `oem_vendor_relationships.md` — vendor concentration, awards, program changes, AI vendor ecosystem
  - `competition.md` — Ingram Micro, Arrow, Avnet, Pax8, marketplaces, direct, analyst views
  - `ai_distribution_2030_outlook.md` — forecasts for distribution, marketplaces, agentic commerce, AI in B2B sales

Fiscal year ends November 30. Gross billings and the Endpoint/Advanced split are not in the 10-Q and still come from earnings releases and secondary sources; the report labels confidence and lists facts to verify.
