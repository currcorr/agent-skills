## What are TD SYNNEX's latest annual and quarterly revenue, gross billings, non-GAAP operating income, EPS, and segment breakdown?

### Takeaway
FY2025 (ended Nov 30, 2025) revenue was $62.5B (+6.9%) and gross billings were roughly $89B (derived), but FY2026 has accelerated sharply on AI infrastructure: Q3 FY26 (ended Aug 31, 2026) revenue was a record $21.6B (+37.7%), gross billings $31.8B (+40.0%), non-GAAP operating income $736M (+55.1%), non-GAAP diluted EPS $5.68 (+58.7%). Gross margin is falling (6.61% in Q3 FY26 vs 7.22%) because of low-margin AI server (Hyve) and large-infrastructure mix. Since Q1 FY26 the company reports four segments: Americas, Europe, APJ (distribution) and Hyve Solutions.
