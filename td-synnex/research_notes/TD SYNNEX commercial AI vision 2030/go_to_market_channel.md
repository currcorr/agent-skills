## 1. How is the commercial/sales organization structured globally and regionally?

### Takeaway
TD SYNNEX organizes around regional presidents (North America, Europe, APJ, LAC) with Advanced Solutions as a specialist overlay, and management says (June 2026) it segments commercial teams into groups of specialists and customer tiers, reallocating resources as often as monthly. The reported financial split is Distribution plus Hyve Solutions (hyperscaler/ODM), not by region or by sales channel. I could not find a published org chart of inside vs. field vs. vendor business units.

### Cited Findings
- Active reseller base is more than 150,000 customers (VARs, ISVs, system integrators, MSPs and others); roughly 24,000 full-time co-workers at Nov 30, 2025; about 48% of FY2025 revenue came from international operations — [FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1177394/000162828026003598/snx-20251130.htm) (as relayed by search summary)
- About 2,500 technology vendors per the fiscal Q4 2025 results release; 100+ countries per company releases (not confirmed in the 10-K excerpt the search tool saw). Wikipedia and an older profile cite ~1,500 vendors, which appears outdated — [Q4 FY25 release](https://ir.tdsynnex.com/news/news-details/2026/TD-SYNNEX-Reports-Record-Fiscal-2025-Fourth-Quarter-Results/default.aspx); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TD_Synnex)
- Fiscal Q2 2026 call (June 25, 2026): CEO Patrick Zammit described three pillars: (1) omnichannel engagement, digital self-serve "when they want speed" and human "when they want expertise," enabled by PartnerFirst and ML/generative/agentic AI personalization; (2) "we segment our commercial teams in groups of specialists," break the customer base into strategic tiers and "in some cases we reallocate resources monthly"; (3) "we invest in enablement." He said SMB customers are growing "well above market" — [Benzinga Q2 2026 transcript](https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/06/60099131/td-synnex-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript); [Investing.com transcript](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-td-synnex-tops-q2-2026-forecasts-as-ai-demand-surges-93CH-4760952) (pillar wording via search summary; a separate Q1 2026 transcript summary referred to "four strategic pillars," so the number varies by call — [MarketBeat Q1 2026](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-3-31-synnex-co-stock/))
- Leadership: Patrick Zammit CEO since Sept 2024 (previously COO and head of Europe/APJ). Reyna Thompson became President, North America on Dec 1, 2024 (replacing Peter Larocque, who moved to an advisory role); she joined in 1993 and previously led North America Advanced Solutions, which serves "over 12,000 channel partners" through programs and practices; CRN Channel Chief 2022-2024 — [TD SYNNEX release](https://s21.q4cdn.com/109490932/files/doc_news/2024/09/1/TD-SYNNEX-to-Appoint-Reyna-Thompson-President-North-America-2024.pdf); [Channel Drive](https://channeldrive.in/people/td-synnex-to-appoint-reyna-thompson-as-president-north-america/); Zammit background via [Distribution Strategy](https://distributionstrategy.com/?p=8771)
- Advanced Solutions (cloud, security, data/AI, data center) is a distinct North America business unit led by Thompson before her promotion; programs under it include CyberSolv, CloudSolv and Public Sector — [Channel Drive](https://channeldrive.in/people/td-synnex-to-appoint-reyna-thompson-as-president-north-america/)
- Public Sector: Wendy Welch named SVP Public Sector Sales (May 2026), leading Federal and SLED including DLT Solutions; company cites 75+ years of combined public-sector service across federal, state/local, education, public health and utilities — [Business Wire, May 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260512914047/en/TD-SYNNEX-Accelerates-Investment-in-U.S.-Public-Sector-Business-at-Annual-Red-White-You-Event)
- Partner community leadership: PartnerLINK led by Kristi Kirby, SVP North American Communities (April 2025). Earlier (2021-22) CommunitySolv was led by Sammy Kinlaw, SVP of Customer Communities — [Channel Insider](https://www.channelinsider.com/news-and-trends/td-synnex-specialized-community-structure/); [Channel Futures](https://channelfutures.com/distribution/td-synnex-vp-discusses-unifying-partner-communities-post-merger)
- Europe: Zammit publicly acknowledged missing "some important cybersecurity vendors" in Europe ("We're working on it") and warned UK partners of possible "pandemic levels" kit shortages in 2026 due to AI demand — [IT Channel Oxygen](https://itchanneloxygen.com/td-synnex-ceo-on-european-portfolio-gaps-exertis-rightsizing-and-kit-shortages/)
- Reporting structure: Hyve Solutions is a wholly owned subsidiary doing design/manufacture for hyperscalers (at least one program with each of the top five US hyperscalers); financial reporting separates Distribution and Hyve — [Distribution Strategy, June 2026](https://distributionstrategy.com/2026/06/td-synnex-doubles-down-on-ai-hyperscalers-and-global-partnerships/)

Flagship events are Inspire (North America, Greenville SC, Oct 7-9, 2026 per a listing; Inspire LAC 2026 with ~700 leaders).

### Cited Findings

- Partner Loyalty program (North America, May 13, 2025, unveiled at High-Growth Conference, Los Angeles): tiered, no extra fee to eligible partners prioritizing growth and Advanced Solutions; benefits include training/certification, travel credit for events, StreamOne account credit, and special interest rates or extended terms via TD SYNNEX Capital and Amplify; extended to other partner types later in 2025; part of PartnerFirst — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250513461477/en/TD-SYNNEX-Accelerates-Innovation-and-Growth-Through-New-Partner-Loyalty-Program); [Business of Tech critique](https://businessof.tech/?p=11243)

## 5. How does TD SYNNEX monetize beyond product margin (services, financing, marketing, data)?

### Takeaway
Monetization levers include TD SYNNEX Capital financing (Amplify short-term credit, 12-60 month payments, Tech-as-a-Service in UK), lifecycle/IT asset services (Shyft Global Services, Renew with Cordon Group in Europe), Hyve design/manufacturing and supply chain services, partner marketing/campaign tools and data-driven PartnerFirst analytics. Vendor-funded rebates, incentives and price protection are central to gross margin economics.

### Cited Findings
- Amplify (US, Sept 2023): partners with active net-terms lines or floor-plan relationships choose 45/60/75/90-day terms; led by Michael Hennig, VP financial solutions — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230928640010/en/TD-SYNNEX-Capital-Introduces-New-Short-Term-Credit-Program-to-Empower-Partner-Growth); [Channel Futures](https://channelfutures.com/distribution/td-synnex-rolls-out-new-short-term-credit-program)
- TD SYNNEX Capital: long-term payments 12-60 months (monthly/quarterly/annual) for any IT product or service; UK Tech-as-a-Service funded up front for full term, "eliminating credit risk" for the partner, first step of a European rollout — [ChannelWeb UK](https://channelweb.co.uk/news/4112793/td-synnex-capital-aims-eliminate-credit-risk-partners-flexible-finance-solutions); [Channel Futures](https://channelfutures.com/channel-business/td-synnex-offers-flexible-financing-with-new-financial-services)
- Third-party floor-plan lenders: fees subsidized by OEMs or paid by TD SYNNEX — [FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1177394/000162828026003598/snx-20251130.htm) (via search summary)
- Lifecycle: Shyft Global Services (procurement to disposal); Renew expanded in Europe with Cordon Group (trade-in, certified data wiping, ITAD, refurbishment), July 2025 — [PCR](https://pcr-online.biz/2025/07/07/td-synnex-lifecycle-with-cordon-group/)
- Hyve: co-design through board manufacturing to full rack integration; NVIDIA HGX design partner; adding 1M+ sq ft US manufacturing space; liquid-cooled networking racks expected in production 1H FY2027 — [Distribution Strategy](https://distributionstrategy.com/2026/06/td-synnex-doubles-down-on-ai-hyperscalers-and-global-partnerships/)
- Vendor-funded economics (10-K language): sell price set by supply and demand; OEM incentive and rebate programs are "important determinants of the final sales price"; cost of revenue is net of incentives, rebates, price protection; OEMs give "limited price protection and return rights"; volume rebates depend on sales volume and customer breadth and growth-based rebates get harder to earn at scale; supplier finance programs give TD SYNNEX better payment terms — [FY2022 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1177394/000117739423000010/snx-20221130.htm); [FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1177394/000162828026003598/snx-20251130.htm)
- Vendor-sponsored programs: Destination AI Practice Accelerator cohorts are vendor-sponsored (Intel/Iterate.ai); HPE "Unleash AI" solutions expanded June 2026 — [Chartmill](https://www.chartmill.com/news/external/45974/bizwire-2026-6-16-td-synnex-expands-hpe-unleash-ai-solutions-to-accelerate-partner-delivery-of-enterprise-ai-solutions)
- Partner marketing: PartnerFirst campaign tools (see section 3). The brief's "marketing-as-a-service" brand was not found.

### Inferences
- Because gross margin (6.61% GAAP in FQ3 2026, down from 7.22%) is falling with Hyve and software mix, financing, services and vendor programs matter more as margin offsets, but their size is undisclosed.

### Gaps
- No revenue/margin contribution for services, financing, marketing services or data monetization; MDF specifics not found; "Intelligent Integration" and "Practice Builder" outside Destination AI not found; no public number for inventory financing volumes.
