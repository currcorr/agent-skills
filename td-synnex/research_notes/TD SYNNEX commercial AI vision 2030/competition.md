## How does TD SYNNEX compare to Ingram Micro and Arrow on revenue, margin, geographic footprint, and digital platforms?

### Takeaway
TD SYNNEX is the largest IT distributor by revenue (Omdia ranks it #1, Ingram #2, Arrow #3 for 2025), and in its latest quarter it grew faster (+31%) than Ingram (+13.6%) and Arrow ECS (+14%), but much of its growth comes from the Hyve hyperscale/ODM business. Gross margins of the two big distributors are close (TD SYNNEX 6.84% vs Ingram 6.60%); Arrow ECS has lower sales but a different mix and its operating income fell 12% in Q2.

## What is the global IT distribution market size and TD SYNNEX's share?

### Takeaway
Estimates of market size vary by publisher and definition: Canalys/Omdia cited US$463B for 2024 (an earlier version said US$452B); Omdia's Distributor 250 totals $389.9B revenue and $34.8B gross profit for 2025; one German analysis cites $531B. On Omdia's top-250 base TD SYNNEX holds roughly 16% (author-calculated), and the top 15 hold ~61-73% depending on source and denominator.

### Cited Findings
- Canalys (part of Omdia) estimated the global IT distribution market at US$463B in 2024, with the top 15 distributors at US$287B (>61%); an earlier version of the same piece gave US$452B. — [Omdia/Canalys "Next for technology distribution" (June 2025)](https://omdia.tech.informa.com/blogs/2025/jun/next-for-technology-distribution); [Canalys Forum EMEA copy](https://canalys-forum-emea.canalys.com/insights/next-for-technology-distribution)

### Inferences
- TD SYNNEX share, author-calculated, mixed periods and bases: ~12.6% of Canalys' $463B (FY24 revenue $58.45B); ~16.0% of Omdia's $389.9B top-250 revenue (FY25 revenue $62.51B); ~11.8% of the $531B figure. ## What are the biggest structural threats to two-tier distribution (marketplaces, direct, consumption models)?

### Takeaway
The most-cited threat is hyperscaler marketplaces, but Omdia/Canalys data show they are still small relative to distribution and increasingly flow through channel partners and distributors; the more tangible pressures are vendor-direct/consumption models, supply-constrained hardware pushing mix to software/cloud, and large VARs buying direct with higher gross margins than distributors.

### Cited Findings
- Omdia forecasts total hyperscaler marketplace spend of $163B per year by 2030; channel partners' share of marketplace revenue was 37% in 2025, expected to reach 50% by 2027 and 59% by 2030. — [IT Channel Oxygen (Canalys)](https://itchanneloxygen.com/channel-to-pocket-59-of-hyperscaler-marketplace-spend-by-2030-canalys); [Channel Dive](https://www.channeldive.com/news/old-school-distribution-channels-dwarf-hyperscaler-marketplaces-omdia/816270/)
- Omdia: Distributor 250 revenue in 2025 was ~5.7x the combined hyperscaler marketplace opportunity; Jay McBain says marketplaces transact less than 1% of the tech/telco industry despite strong growth. Most partner marketplace transactions still traveled the traditional distribution route. — [DutchITChannel](https://www.dutchitchannel.nl/research/728218/it-distributeurs-domineren-de-marketplace-deals); [Channel Dive](https://www.channeldive.com/news/old-school-distribution-channels-dwarf-hyperscaler-marketplaces-omdia/816270/); [IT Channel Oxygen "6 bold claims" ](https://itchanneloxygen.com/every-partner-should-be-investing-in-hyperscaler-marketplaces-6-bold-claims-from-omdias-big-briefing/)
- Earlier Canalys headline: AWS Marketplace "will be a top 5 distributor soon" (date not captured). — [IT Channel Oxygen](https://itchanneloxygen.com/aws-marketplace-will-be-a-top-5-distributor-soon-canalys/)

### Inferences
- The marketplace threat is currently less "replacement" and more "re-routing": channel share of marketplace spend is forecast to rise from 37% to 59%, and distributors are positioning as the multi-party private offer layer. Risk is concentrated in margin: marketplace listing fees paid to hyperscalers (not found in sources) and netted-down revenue compress distributor gross margin per dollar of billings.
- Vendor-direct/consumption models and netted-down software revenue already show up in the numbers: TD SYNNEX net revenue is ~29% below gross billings; ScanSource says netted-down recurring revenue lifts margin %. Distributors report billings alongside revenue to show growth.
- Large VARs (CDW, Insight, SHI) earn ~20-22% gross margin vs ~6.6-6.8% for TD SYNNEX/Ingram, but they are also distributors' customers; their direct-buy behavior is a threat mainly for hyperscaler/ODM and large-account deals (the segment where Hyve operates).

### Gaps
- Searches returned no quantitative data on Amazon Business, Dell/HPE direct share of channel sales, or vendor-direct program shifts (e.g., Microsoft CSP changes, Cisco direct). These were not covered in available results.
- Hyperscaler marketplace fee structures and distributor take-rates not found.
- Forrester and IDC views on structural threats not found.

## What do analysts say about the role of distributors in 2030?

### Takeaway
Omdia/Canalys's central view is that distributors are resilient and evolving into platform-centric, data-powered orchestrators in a many-to-many ecosystem, with human relationships still a differentiator; Gartner's published views focus on agentic AI disruption to IT providers broadly, and no quantified 2030 forecast for distributors from Gartner, IDC or Forrester was found.

### Cited Findings
- Omdia: distributors are shifting from box-moving to a "platform-centric, data-powered orchestration model" at the center of a many-to-many network of vendors, cloud providers, hyperscalers, MSPs and consultants; differentiation lies in aggregating complex solutions, AI automation, API integration, financing, and lifecycle management. — [DutchITChannel](https://www.dutchitchannel.nl/research/728218/it-distributeurs-domineren-de-marketplace-deals)
- Omdia (June 2025): human engagement and personal relationships remain a fundamental differentiator even as platform automation grows. — [Omdia blog](https://omdia.tech.informa.com/blogs/2025/jun/next-for-technology-distribution)
- Omdia: channel partners to take 59% of $163B annual hyperscaler marketplace spend by 2030; "every partner should be investing in hyperscaler marketplaces." — [IT Channel Oxygen](https://itchanneloxygen.com/channel-to-pocket-59-of-hyperscaler-marketplace-spend-by-2030-canalys); [IT Channel Oxygen](https://itchanneloxygen.com/every-partner-should-be-investing-in-hyperscaler-marketplaces-6-bold-claims-from-omdias-big-briefing/)
