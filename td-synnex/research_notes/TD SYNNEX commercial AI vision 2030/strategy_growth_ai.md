## 1. What does TD SYNNEX say its strategic priorities are for 2025-2027 and beyond? (incl. CEO Zammit's priorities)

### Takeaway
The last formal investor day found is March 2022 (four pillars: end-to-end portfolio, high-growth technologies, digital transformation, global footprint). Under CEO Patrick Zammit (CEO since Sept 1, 2025, per one source; see Gaps on the date) the public emphasis is AI/data-center infrastructure (Hyve and Advanced Solutions), AI PCs, security/compliance, digital partner tooling (PartnerFirst/Digital Bridge) and making distribution more valuable to vendors. I found no investor day after 2022.

- March 29, 2022 virtual Investor Day (CEO Rich Hume): plan to grow position and addressable market through core and high-growth technologies; "medium term" defined as 3-4 years, with revenue growth of 6-7% CAGR, total shareholder return of 15-20%, and non-GAAP operating margin of about 3%. — [SEC 8-K investor day release (via search summary)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1177394/000117739422000022/investordaypressrelease1fi.htm); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220329005663/en/TD-SYNNEX-Hosts-2022-Virtual-Investor-Day-and-Outlines-Key-Strategic-Growth-Initiatives-and-Medium-Term-Outlook/)
