# How AI (generative and agentic) is expected to reshape IT distribution, channel sales and B2B commercial organizations by 2030 (relevance to TD SYNNEX)

**Method caveat for the report writer.** WebFetch was blocked by the network egress proxy for every primary source tried (Gartner, Omdia, Business Wire, Digital Commerce 360, IDC). All findings below come from WebSearch result summaries that cite the URLs listed, not from reading the primary documents. Where a figure appears only in secondary or vendor coverage, it is flagged. Date of research: October 2026. Forecast = a named analyst's projection; Current = reported actuals or announcements.

## 1. What do analysts forecast for IT distribution and the channel in 2028-2030 (size, mix, marketplace share)?

### Takeaway
Current IT distribution is large and concentrating (about $531B in 2025 per Omdia, up 15%), while the fastest-growing flow is hyperscaler marketplace software ($30B in 2024 to $163B by 2030, Omdia), with partners expected to transact close to 60% of it. I found no public forecast of total distribution revenue for 2028-2030; the 2030 numbers that exist concern marketplaces and AI services.

### Cited Findings
- Current: Omdia analysts calculate global IT distribution sales grew 15% to about $531B in 2025 — [Dutch IT Channel (citing Omdia)](https://www.dutchitchannel.nl/research/757065/it-distributie-groeit-naar-531-miljard-dollar); [ITreseller.ch](https://www.itreseller.ch/Artikel/105976/IT-Distribution_waechst_kraeftig_-_Also_legt_am_staerksten_zu.html)
- Current: Omdia expects the 15 largest global distributors to strengthen their position further as vendors streamline distributor networks — [Dutch IT Channel](https://www.dutchitchannel.nl/research/757065/it-distributie-groeit-naar-531-miljard-dollar)
- Earlier Canalys data: 2024 IT distribution was about $463B, and the top 15 distributors held $287B, over 61% of sales — [IT Channel Oxygen / Omdia-Canalys coverage](https://itchanneloxygen.com/market-data/page/2/) (as summarised by the search; the exact article was not individually confirmed)
- Forecast (Omdia, Oct 2025): enterprise software sales through hyperscaler marketplaces (AWS, Microsoft, Google Cloud) grow from about $30B in 2024 to $163B by 2030, a 29.1% CAGR for 2025-2030 — [Intelligent CIO](https://www.intelligentcio.com/apac/2025/10/07/omdia-forecasts-us163bn-hyperscaler-cloud-marketplace-sales-by-2030/); contradicted on the base year by an article that states $16B (the $30B base is cited by more sources, so treat $16B as the weaker figure) — [Business Wire release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251006925215/en)
- Forecast (Omdia): channel partners will facilitate nearly 60% of all marketplace transactions by 2030; three categories (infrastructure software $10.5B, DevOps $9.1B, business applications $9.1B) make up 63% of spend in the cited breakdown; about $470B in upfront cloud commitments exist across AWS, Azure and Google Cloud — [Intelligent CIO](https://www.intelligentcio.com/apac/2025/10/07/omdia-forecasts-us163bn-hyperscaler-cloud-marketplace-sales-by-2030/)
- Forecast (Canalys, older): hyperscaler marketplace spend $16B in 2023 to $85B in 2028 — [IT Channel Oxygen](https://itchanneloxygen.com/5-partners-reveal-their-hyperscaler-marketplace-strategies). Note the 2030 Omdia figure ($163B) is much larger than the extrapolation of this earlier Canalys path, so the forecast has been revised up sharply; the two use different baselines and scope.
- Forecast (Omdia, 2026): partner-delivered IT growth of 6.7% in 2026 versus 6.3% in 2025; indirect vendors "cannot go alone" despite direct hyperscaler spend — [Omdia, Jan 2026 blog](https://omdia.tech.informa.com/blogs/2026/jan/how-will-changing-it-spending-trends-impact-global-channel-chiefs) (via search summary)
- Distributor-side view: GTDC reports 60% of vendors have direct relationships with hyperscalers and 40% use a hybrid direct plus distributor model; GTDC CEO Frank Vitagliano argues hyperscalers lack resources for mid-market and SMB where channel partners dominate (GTDC is an industry body representing distributors, so this is an interested party) — [IT Europa](https://iteuropa.com/news/conversation-frank-vitagliano-distributions-biggest-transformation); [IT Europa on marketplaces](https://www.iteuropa.com/news/digital-marketplaces-and-hyperscaler-partnerships-are-reshaping-distribution)
- Current, TD SYNNEX: CEO Patrick Zammit said StreamOne has 30,000 active partners and 500,000 end users — [ChannelWeb](https://channelweb.co.uk/news-network/2025/td-synnex-ceo-patrick-zammit-on-streamone-cloud-marketplace-growth-tariffs-and-broadcom-ceo-hock-tan-s-channel-commitment)
- Current, Ingram Micro Q2 2026: gross margin 6.60%; GPU/AI infrastructure sales more than doubled year over year but at lower margins — [MarketBeat call highlights](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/ingram-micro-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-07-31/)

### Inferences
- The marketplace trajectory (about 5.4x in six years) means a rising share of software resale becomes "marketplace-transacted"; the ~60% channel-facilitated share is the key number for whether distributors with marketplace-enablement (StreamOne, Xvantage Cloud, Pax8, ArrowSphere) capture or lose that flow.
- AI infrastructure growth raises revenue but dilutes distributor gross margin (see Ingram 6.60%), so value-added AI services and software matter for the mix.

### Gaps
- No public (non-paywalled) Omdia, Canalys, IDC or Gartner forecast of total IT distribution revenue, or distributor mix, for 2028-2030 found.
- Marketplace share of total software or cloud sales (as a percentage of the whole) was not found; only absolute marketplace dollars. Gartner marketplace-share figures were not found.
- Full Omdia reports are paywalled; Forrester channel predictions were not retrieved.

## 2. What are the credible forecasts for AI PC and AI server/infrastructure demand through 2030?

### Takeaway
AI infrastructure is forecast to keep compounding (IDC: $758B of AI infrastructure spend by 2029, accelerated servers more than 95% of AI server spend; Gartner: $2.67B-type totals are actually $2.67 trillion of AI spending in 2026 with $1.48T infrastructure). AI PCs are a majority of 2026 shipments by most trackers (Gartner 55%, Counterpoint 59%), but a memory shortage is cutting total PC units in 2026 (IDC -11.3%), which complicates the device story for distributors.

### Cited Findings
- Forecast (IDC, Oct 2025): AI infrastructure spending reaches $758B by 2029; accelerated servers exceed 95% of AI server spend by 2029 with a 42% five-year CAGR; Q2 2025 spend was $82B (+166% YoY); regional CAGRs PRC 41.5%, US 40.5%, EMEA 17.3%, APJ 14.3% — [IDC press release](https://www.idc.com/resource-center/press-releases/artificial-intelligence-infrastructure-spending-to-reach-758bn-usd-mark-by-2029-according-to-idc/); [Techstrong.ai](https://techstrong.ai/articles/ai-infrastructure-spending-surges-past-82-billion-as-market-eyes-758-billion-by-2029/)
- Forecast (Gartner, Sept 2026 update): worldwide AI spending grows 49.5% to about $2.67 trillion in 2026 (from about $1.79T in 2025), with AI infrastructure about $1.48T; this was raised from $2.60T and $1.43T in May — [Campus Technology](https://campustechnology.com/articles/2026/09/21/infrastructure-accounts-for-more-than-half-of-worldwide-ai-spending.aspx)
- Forecast (Gartner, earlier): January 2026 forecast of $2.5T for 2026 and $3.34T in 2027; May 2026 update put 2027 total at $3.49T with infrastructure $1.89T — [Gartner press release, Jan 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-1-15-gartner-says-worldwide-ai-spending-will-total-2-point-5-trillion-dollars-in-2026); [ARN](https://www.arnnet.com.au/article/4173416/global-ai-spend-to-hit-us2-59t-in-2026-gartner.html)

- Forecast (Canalys, March 2024, now dated): AI-capable PCs 18% (48M) of 2024 shipments, 40% (over 100M) in 2025, 205M in 2028 (44% CAGR 2024-2028) — [Canalys](https://canalys.com:443/newsroom/ai-pc-market-2024). Actual 2025 outcomes versus this forecast not found.
- Forecast (IDC, Feb 2024, dated): AI PCs nearly 60% of shipments (167M+) by 2027 — [EMSNow](https://www.emsnow.com/?p=40118). Definitions of "AI PC" differ across firms, explaining the spread.

### Gaps
- No credible 2030-specific AI PC share forecast from IDC, Gartner or Canalys/Omdia found (latest are 2027-2029). No Omdia AI server market forecast to 2030 found.
- Edge AI-specific forecast from a tier-one analyst not found; third-party figures vary widely by definition.
- Sovereign AI numbers are secondary only.

## 3. How are B2B sales organizations using AI agents today and what is projected (Gartner: % of B2B sales interactions via AI by 2028, etc.)?

### Takeaway
Gartner's headline predictions are that AI agents will intermediate over $15 trillion of B2B spending by 2028 and 60% of B2B seller work will run through generative AI conversational interfaces by 2028, but buyer surveys show resistance (Gartner: 75% of B2B buyers prefer human interaction by 2030) and Gartner also expects over 40% of agentic AI projects to be cancelled by end-2027. Evidence so far is mostly forecasts and vendor surveys, with adoption still early.

### Cited Findings
- Forecast (Gartner, IT Symposium/Xpo Oct 2025): AI agents will intermediate more than $15 trillion in B2B spending by 2028 — [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/11/28/gartner-ai-agents-15-trillion-in-b2b-purchases-by-2028/); [MarketScale](https://marketscale.com/industries/retail/zero-click-commerce-arrives-ai-agents-set-to-intermediate-15-trillion-in-b2b-purchases-by-2028/)
- Conflict/uncertainty: many outlets state "90% of B2B purchases handled by AI agents by 2028"; I could not confirm the 90% figure against Gartner's own release, and one blog claims "one in four enterprise software purchases will be made by agents with no human"; also unverified — [Two.inc blog (vendor)](https://www.two.inc/resources/two-cents-blog/ai-agents-will-run-90-of-b2b-buying-by-2028-what-gartners-15-trillion-forecast-means-for-payments-and-credit); [Mindset Online](https://mindsetonline.com/gartner-2028-one-in-four-software-purchases-ai-agents-no-human/). The $15T figure is the more consistent one. Use "$15T intermediated" and treat "90%" as unconfirmed.
- Forecast (Gartner, 2023): by 2028, 60% of B2B seller work will be executed through conversational UIs via generative AI — [DestinationCRM](https://www.destinationcrm.com/Articles/CRM-News/CRM-Featured-Articles/Gartner-60-of-Sales-to-Be-Carried-Out-by-AI-160659.aspx)
- Forecast (Gartner, 2025): by 2030, 75% of B2B buyers will prefer sales experiences that prioritize human interaction over AI; another secondary source cites a 2026 Gartner survey where 67% of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free experience (these partially conflict) — [DestinationCRM](https://www.destinationcrm.com/Articles/CRM-Insights/Insight/Buyers-Are-Pushing-Back-Against-AI-171784.aspx); [Komo glossary](https://komo.ai/glossary/generative-ai-for-sales)
- Forecast (Gartner, June 2025): over 40% of agentic AI projects cancelled by end-2027; only about 130 of thousands of agentic vendors are real ("agent washing"); at least 15% of day-to-day work decisions autonomous by 2028 (0% in 2024); 33% of enterprise software apps include agentic AI by 2028 — [Gartner press release](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027)
- Current (Forrester via a vendor-cited source): 94% of B2B buyers use generative AI during purchasing; Forrester still calls autonomous B2B buying rare and early-stage — [Two.inc blog](https://www.two.inc/resources/two-cents-blog/ai-agents-will-run-90-of-b2b-buying-by-2028-what-gartners-15-trillion-forecast-means-for-payments-and-credit); [Stellagent](https://stellagent.ai/insights/visa-lianlian-loopxpay-b2b-agentic-payment)
- Current (McKinsey 2026 B2B Pulse, about 4,000 buyers and sellers, 13 countries, via trade article): 71% of growth champions raised AI spend significantly in 2026 versus 25% of laggards; value comes from rebuilding workflows end to end, not point tools — [MarketScale summary](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/marketing-tech/b2b-growth-leaders-are-three-times-more-likely-to-double-ai-investment-and-agentic-workflows-are-why.md)
- Current (McKinsey via Distribution Strategy): 90% of distributors surveyed have AI initiatives, only 11% fully adopted — [Distribution Strategy](https://distributionstrategy.com/2026/09/distributors-move-from-buying-ai-software-to-buying-ai-companies-technology-and-talent/)
- Vendor-sourced stats (low reliability): 89% of B2B revenue organizations use AI; AI users 3.7x more likely to hit quota (Mutiny, sells AI tools) — [Mutiny](https://www.mutinyhq.com/blog/how-are-b2b-sales-teams-using-ai-in-2026)

### Inferences
- The tension between "$15T intermediated by agents" and "75% prefer humans" suggests agents mainly handle research, quoting, reorder and routine procurement while humans stay in complex, high-value deals. That favors distributors that combine agent-ready data with human technical expertise.
- Gartner's 40% cancellation call implies a shake-out; reference-quality deployments by incumbents will matter.

### Gaps
- Gartner's primary 2028 B2B press release was not retrieved; the exact wording ("intermediate" versus "handle") and whether 90% is a real Gartner claim is unresolved.
- No primary Forrester, BCG numbers on B2B sales agents retrieved (BCG only via a CMO-focused trade summary).
- The % of B2B sales interactions by AI by 2028 beyond the 60% seller-work and $15T statistics was not found.

## 4. What is agentic commerce and what protocols/standards matter (MCP, A2A, agentic payments)?

### Takeaway
Agentic commerce is AI agents researching, negotiating and buying on a principal's behalf. The emerging stack is layered: MCP (tools/data), A2A (agent-to-agent), and payment/checkout protocols (AP2, ACP, UCP, Visa Trusted Agent Protocol, Mastercard Agent Pay). McKinsey sizes the global opportunity at $3-5T by 2030, mostly retail.

### Cited Findings
- Forecast (McKinsey, Oct 2025): agentic commerce could reach $3-5 trillion globally by 2030, about $1T in the US (orchestrated retail revenue); McKinsey argues B2B adoption is slower because "delegation is institutional" (procurement policies, budget owners, risk, legal) but scales once authority is set — [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/10/20/mckinsey-forecast-5-trillion-agentic-commerce-sales-2030/); [Stellagent](https://stellagent.ai/insights/agentic-commerce-market-size-forecast-2030). A "$1.2T B2B procurement" slice circulates in a Shopify-agency blog; I could not verify it in McKinsey sources, so do not use it — [Tenten](https://tenten.co/shopify/agentic-commerce-market-size/)
- MCP is the tool/data layer; Anthropic donated MCP to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) in December 2025, with OpenAI, Block, Google, Microsoft, AWS, Cloudflare and Bloomberg among supporters — [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/model-context-protocol-donated-to-linux-foundation-the-tcp-ip-of-agentic-ai/); [Paz.ai](https://www.paz.ai/blog/anthropic-donates-mcp-to-linux-foundation-what-retailers-must-know-now). Secondary sources disagree on whether A2A is hosted by the Linux Foundation directly or as part of that foundation.
- A2A is the agent-to-agent standard (Agent Cards, task delegation) backed by Google and hosted by the Linux Foundation — [Stellagent protocol comparison](https://stellagent.ai/insights/mcp-vs-a2a-vs-ap2-protocol-comparison)
- Payments/checkout: AP2 (Google, Mastercard and 60+ organizations) handles signed-intent authorization; ACP (Stripe and OpenAI) powers ChatGPT Instant Checkout; Google's Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) competes with ACP for checkout — [Agile Brand Guide](https://agilebrandguide.com/wiki/agentic-ai/agentic-commerce-protocols/); [Stellagent](https://stellagent.ai/insights/agentic-ai-protocols-mcp-a2a-ucp)
- Card networks: Mastercard Agent Pay (Agentic Tokens) and Visa Trusted Agent Protocol (Oct 14, 2025, now part of Visa Intelligent Commerce) both use Web Bot Auth for agent authentication — [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/10/16/visa-mastercard-both-launch-agentic-ai-payments-tools/); [Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/secure-agentic-commerce/)
- First B2B example: July 2026, Visa and Lianlian DigiTech completed a live B2B agentic transaction in Greater China via the LoopXPay agent (identify need, compare suppliers, order, pay) — [Stellagent](https://stellagent.ai/insights/visa-lianlian-loopxpay-b2b-agentic-payment) (secondary)
- Marketplace uptake of MCP/A2A: AWS Marketplace "AI Agents and Tools" category (July 2025) launched with 800-900+ listings and flags MCP/A2A support, with natural-language search and deployment on Bedrock AgentCore — [Channel Insider](https://www.channelinsider.com/ai/the-new-aws-marketplace-category-for-ai-agents-and-tools/); [CRN Asia](https://www.crnasia.com/news-network/2025/aws-launches-ai-agents-marketplace-and-bedrock-agentcore-aws); [AWS APN blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/apn/aws-partner-guide-to-ai-agents-and-tools-in-aws-marketplace)
- Pax8 is integrating MCP into its Marketplace commerce APIs and catalog so Microsoft Copilots and agents can transact directly — [Pax8 release (June 2025)](https://www.pax8.com/en-us/news-post/pax8-to-unlock-the-era-of-managed-intelligence-for-smbs/)

### Inferences
- For a distributor, exposing catalog, price, availability, quote and credit APIs through MCP and being a verified agent counterparty is table stakes; machine-readable terms and credit (financing) are where distributors have an edge over pure marketplaces.
- Most agentic-commerce sizing is consumer or retail oriented, so B2B IT figures should lean on Gartner's $15T, with its caveats.

### Gaps
- No analyst forecast of B2B IT-specific agentic transaction volume found. No evidence yet of agent-to-agent IT quoting between a distributor and a reseller at scale.
- Protocol governance details (A2A host) conflict in secondary sources.

## 6. What are the risks to distributors (disintermediation) and the new value pools (AI integration, data, financing, services)?

### Takeaway
Analysts rate resellers and distributors' direct AI services opportunity "low to medium" versus the $267B (title says $276B) partner AI services pool that goes mainly to GSIs, consultants and MSPs, while hyperscaler marketplaces and agent-mediated buying create disintermediation pressure. Offsetting factors: channel partners are expected to facilitate close to 60% of marketplace flow, and mid-market/SMB reach, credit and logistics remain distributor strengths.

### Cited Findings
- Forecast (Canalys/Omdia, Sept 2025, "From GenAI to agents"): partner opportunity for AI and agentic AI services reaches about $267B by 2030 at a 35.3% CAGR; GSIs "very high", consultants/MSPs/MSSPs "high", resellers and distributors "low to medium"; 47% of customers rely on partners for agentic AI — [Omdia/Canalys report page](https://omdia.tech.informa.com/om142368/from-generative-ai-to-agentic-ai-the-us$276-billion-partner-opportunity); [IT Channel Oxygen](https://itchanneloxygen.com/ai-opportunity-for-resellers-and-distributors-low-to-medium-analyst/). Discrepancy: the report URL says US$276B while the search summary quotes US$267B; verify which is correct before publishing.
- Earlier Canalys: generative AI partner services opportunity of $158B by 2027/2028 (sources differ on year) — [Canalys outlook](https://canalys-forum-emea.canalys.com/resources/canalys-outlook-predictions-for-the-technology-industry-in-2024); [IT Channel Oxygen](https://itchanneloxygen.com/?p=10635). Scope differs from the 2030 figure.
- Disintermediation risks: hyperscaler marketplaces described as a major new disintermediation threat while the channel role is "yet to be determined" — [IT Channel Oxygen](https://itchanneloxygen.com/5-partners-reveal-their-hyperscaler-marketplace-strategies); a distribution-industry piece on direct-sale threats — [MDM](https://www.mdm.com/article/featured/featured-blog/the-threat-of-distributor-disintermediation/)
- Counterweights: Omdia says indirect vendors cannot go alone and expects the top 15 distributors to gain share — see Q1 sources; GTDC says hyperscalers lack SMB/mid-market reach — [IT Europa](https://iteuropa.com/news/conversation-frank-vitagliano-distributions-biggest-transformation)

## 7. Workforce implications for sales organizations

### Takeaway
Bain's January 2026 survey (1,125 executives, 18 industries) indicates spending is shifting toward automation and AI and away from adding sales reps, with tech sales hiring growth falling to under 1% a year; evidence on actual headcount cuts is thin.

### Cited Findings
- Current (Bain, Jan 2026): tech sales hiring grew 18% from 2017 to 2022 but under 1% a year since; AI and automation let organizations cover more accounts with less incremental hiring — [Bain B2B Growth Agenda 2026](https://www.bain.com/es-mx/insights/the-new-sales-productivity-equation-b2b-growth-agenda-2026/) (via search summary)
- Current (McKinsey 2026): winners rebuild commercial workflows end to end rather than adding point tools — [MarketScale summary](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/marketing-tech/b2b-growth-leaders-are-three-times-more-likely-to-double-ai-investment-and-agentic-workflows-are-why.md)

- Distributor example: Grainger uses AI to coach sales leaders and identify new contacts (augmentation); Ingram frames its agent as a sales-force briefing tool — see Q5.

### Inferences
- The consistent framing is augmentation and capacity (more accounts per rep, flat headcount) rather than announced layoffs; roles likely to change fastest are inside sales, quoting/deal desk and customer service.
