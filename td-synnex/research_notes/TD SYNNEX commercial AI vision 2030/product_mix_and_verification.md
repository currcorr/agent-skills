## Product mix: PCs, mobile, peripherals, software/cloud, servers/storage/networking, security, Hyve, services

### Takeaway
TD SYNNEX discloses only three product-level buckets: Endpoint Solutions, Advanced Solutions (both inside Distribution) and, from FY2026, Hyve as a separate segment. On a gross-billings basis for FY2025 my estimate is roughly Endpoint ~40%, Advanced ex-Hyve ~47-48% and Hyve ~13%. On a reported-revenue basis Endpoint is likely a majority (~54%) because software, cloud and security are booked net. Category splits below those buckets (PCs vs mobile vs peripherals; software vs hardware inside Advanced) are low-confidence analyst-style estimates with no company anchor.

- Endpoint Solutions (Tech Data legacy definition, FY2020 10-K): PC systems, mobile phones and accessories, printers, peripherals, supplies, endpoint technology software and consumer electronics. Advanced Solutions: data center technologies such as storage, networking, servers, advanced technology software, converged and hyper-converged infrastructure. — [Tech Data FY2020 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/790703/000079070320000006/fy2010kdocumentclassic.htm) (as summarised by search tool)

## Endpoint Solutions vs Advanced Solutions: FY2024 and FY2025 shares, growth, definitions and Hyve reporting

### Takeaway
Full-year FY2025 portfolio dollars/growth were not found; quarterly data show Endpoint growing 8-13% and Advanced 7-17% (with Hyve) in FY2025.
